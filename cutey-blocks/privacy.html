Cutey Blocks Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

In short: Cutey Blocks has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or any other personal details. Your scores, coins and settings stay on your device. We do not collect, receive or sell any personal data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

Who we are

Cutey Blocks is made by DoubleD-O ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your privacy, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What the game stores on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your phone or tablet only:

  • your best scores (today, this week, this month, this year and all time);
  • your coin balance and the day you last collected the daily reward;
  • the theme you picked.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the game. Deleting the app deletes it. On Android, if you have turned on Google's device backup, Android may include it in your backup, which Google keeps under your Google account.

Advertising

Cutey Blocks is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, an advertising service run by Google. Some ads are optional: for example, you can choose to watch an ad to collect the daily free coins. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob software in the app may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (the Android advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, when you allow it);
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information about how the ads perform.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive this information from Google in a form that identifies you.

Your ad choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads can use your data for personalized advertising, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button at the top of the game's Home screen. If you do not consent, you may still see ads that are not personalized.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used to track you across apps. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it is not shared. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy > Ads (the exact name depends on your device).
  • In US states with privacy laws such as California, we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves. To limit how Google's ads use your device data, use the device settings above.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own, and no servers of our own that receive data from the game.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • No chat or any way to talk to other players.

Children

Cutey Blocks is a family-friendly game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game keeps no personal information on our side, there is nothing for us to delete; a parent can reset the device's advertising ID as described above, or contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with any concern.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as to access, correct or delete it. We do not hold personal data about you, so a request to us will usually have nothing to return. For data collected by Google's ads, use the ad choices above or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. You can always contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL], and you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players.
